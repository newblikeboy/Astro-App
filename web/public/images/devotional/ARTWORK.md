# Devotional illustration provenance

These original illustrations were generated for Mishra 4 You with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. They are illustrations, not photographs of historic artifacts, saints or temples. They carry no Commons source or license claim.

Each final image is locally hosted, at most 512 pixels per side and 100 KB. Packaging only resizes and encodes the image; it does not crop, retouch or alter its content. The complete prompts are in [ARTWORK-PROMPTS.json](ARTWORK-PROMPTS.json).

The original Commons image sources and their licenses remain in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md). Unreplaced photos and historical paintings retain those credits.
